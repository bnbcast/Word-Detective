Can you spot an AI by its words?

Word Detective compares how often each word shows up in AI-written answers versus human-written ones, then ranks the words that give the writer away.

Start exploring

How it works

Three ideas, no heavy math required.

1. Two events. AI means “this word was written by an AI”; Human means “written by a person.” W means “the word is the one we’re studying.”

2. Count every occurrence. For each group we ask: out of all the words in these answers, what fraction is our word? That gives P(W | AI) and P(W | Human).

3. Smooth the extremes. Laplace smoothing pretends we saw one extra “hit” and one extra “miss” in each group, so no word is ever literally impossible.

The recipe is identical for every word; only the word being counted changes.
